# Rebuild Your Social Circle Through Istanbul Volunteering

The quickest way to build a new circle in Istanbul this winter is to combine one weekly team activity with one big date to work toward. First, join an **Adım Adım charity-running group** now and train toward the **18 April 2027 Istanbul Half Marathon**; the groups run on Saturdays in Belgrad Forest and on Wednesday evenings on the Asian side. Second, apply to the **Istanbul 2027 European Games volunteer programme before its stated 15 January 2027 deadline**. For a second weekly commitment, Turkish speakers should look at **LÖSEV**, which approves online applications within 24 hours, holds intro meetings every 15 days and has a volunteer choir and socials, or **TEGV**, where you run weekly children's activities after a one-day live training. Anyone on the European side can also join **Çorbada Tuzun Olsun**, whose volunteers, mostly students and office workers, hand out soup around Taksim's Gezi Park six evenings a week. Language is the main filter. An English-language expat guide says outright that most volunteering in Istanbul is in Turkish. Non-Turkish speakers should therefore start with the European Games, animal sanctuaries such as **Kurtaran Ev** (walk in any day, 12:00–17:00), **Istanbul&I**, **International Women of Istanbul** (women only) and two English-language Rotary clubs. Three names that still appear in older guides should come off your list. **Ahbap** has been run by a state-appointed trustee since July 2026. The **Tarlabaşı Community Center** was sealed in 2024, and in 2026 an appeals court ruled that it had legally ceased to exist; the case is now at the Court of Cassation. **Small Projects Istanbul** says on its own website that it has closed. One caveat applies to everything below: the researchers could not open most organisations' websites directly and worked from search-engine summaries, so check hours, contacts and deadlines before you go.

## Six places to start, ranked by how fast they build a crew

The options below are ranked on two things. The first is how good each one is for meeting the same people again and again: it should repeat every week or every season, put you in a team rather than alone, and have a real community around it. The second is whether you can start now, in late November 2026, rather than waiting for a call that may never come. That rules out several worthy options. Audiobook recording for blind readers is done mostly **at home and alone**, for example: one programme's volunteers have recorded 36,000 books remotely ([TRT Haber](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/gorme-engelliler-icin-36-bin-kitap-seslendirdiler-401741.html)). It also pushes formats with built-in group time toward the top. One gap is worth admitting up front: no source described what actually happens after shifts (who goes for tea, who stays in touch). The rankings therefore lean on structure (weekly sessions, cohort training, teams) rather than first-hand reports of post-activity socialising.

| Rank | Option | What you do and time needed | Language | Side / area | Can you start now? | First step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adım Adım charity running | Weekly group runs with volunteer coaches; raise money for an NGO you choose; race on 18 Apr 2027 | Mixed | European (Belgrad Forest, Sat) and Asian (Wed evening) | Yes: the winter is the run-up to April | Email the beginners' group; pick a cause on ipk.adimadim.org |
| 2 | Çorbada Tuzun Olsun | Cook, pack and hand out soup to homeless people, evenings Mon–Sat | Mixed (Turkish-run; inferred) | European: Taksim / Gezi Park, Beyoğlu | Yes: active in Oct 2026 | Contact via its website or Instagram; confirm the meeting time |
| 3 | Istanbul 2027 European Games | Venue teams during 16–27 Jun 2027, plus training beforehand | English-friendly (English entry point; language rules not published) | City-wide venues | Apply by 15 Jan 2027 | volunteer.2027istanbul.org only |
| 4 | LÖSEV | Awareness campaigns, events, choir, workplace representative | Turkish needed | Both sides | Yes: online approval within 24 hours | "Gönüllü Ol" form at losev.org.tr |
| 5 | TEGV activity volunteer | Recurring children's sessions (IT, maths, reading, art, English) at a learning unit or education park | Turkish needed (English-teaching niche) | Both sides | Yes, but allow weeks for paperwork | tegv.org/gonullu-ol |
| 6 | Kurtaran Ev (or the Yedikule or Kadıköy shelters) | Dog walking and animal care, half a day | Mixed | European (Hadımköy, far west; Yedikule in Fatih); Asian (Kadıköy) | Yes: walk-ins every day | Just turn up 12:00–17:00; optional form |

A realistic first two weeks would look like this. Submit the European Games application, or at least check whether the form is open. Fill in the LÖSEV form so you catch the next intro meeting. Go to one Saturday Adım Adım session. Spend one evening on a Taksim soup round. By early December you will know which of these feels like your people.

### Adım Adım turns a race date into a weekly running crew

Adım Adım was founded in **March 2008** and is run entirely by volunteers. Members meet **every Saturday in Belgrad Forest** for group training and hold **Wednesday-evening sessions on the Anatolian side**. Beginners ("çaylak") train weekly with volunteer coaches, and you do not need to be fast ([Adım Adım](https://adimadim.org/); [Adım Adım SSS](https://adimadim.org/sss/)). Some of these pages are older, so confirm current meeting points. The beginners' group can be reached at **caylaklar@adimadim.org**, and the organisation describes itself as people doing good through sport rather than a running club ([Magma Dergisi](https://mail.magmadergisi.com/yasam-haberleri/adim-adim-iyilik-pesinde-kos); [Gönüllü Hareketi](https://www.gonulluhareketi.org/adim-adim)). Each runner raises money for an NGO through the İyilik Peşinde Koş platform. At the 2025 Istanbul Marathon, **67 NGOs raised about 166 million TL** this way ([Ekonomist](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/haberler/istanbul-maratonu-nda-iyilik-rekoru-kirildi-68076)). Teams can be large: **820 people ran for Darüşşafaka alone** in 2025 ([Darüşşafaka](https://www.darussafaka.org/bagislariniz/bagis-kanallari/yardimseverlik-kosusu)). The next target is the **22nd Istanbul Half Marathon on Sunday 18 April 2027**, which starts at Yenikapı and has 10K and 21K races capped at 10,000 runners each ([RaceTürkiye](https://www.raceturkiye.com/tr/race/istanbul-yari-maratonu-2027)). Race entry is separate from the fundraising campaign, and campaigns open roughly **four weeks before the race** ([ÖRAV](https://www.orav.org.tr/yardimseverlik-kosusu)), so expect them around late March. That gives you about five months of shared weekly training, which is the main reason this ranks first. Many of the causes can feed a second commitment later: Türkiye Down Sendromu Derneği, the Spastic Children's Foundation (TSÇV), TOFD, Koruncuk, ÇYDD, Tohum Otizm and Eşpedal all recruit charity runners ([İPK – TDSD](https://ipk.adimadim.org/nonprofit/view?id=27327); [İPK – TSÇV](https://ipk.adimadim.org/nonprofit/view?id=27353); [Tohum](https://tohumotizm.org.tr/nasil-destek-olabilirsiniz/yardimseverlik-kosulari/)). The organisation is Turkish-led, but running needs little language, the Half Marathon has an English charity-run page ([Istanbul Half Marathon](https://istanbulyarimaratonu.com/en/charity-run/)), and an "Istanbul Expat Runners" Facebook group exists ([Made Easier Guides](https://madeeasierguides.com/groups-and-associations-in-istanbul.html)). That is why it is tagged mixed.

### Çorbada Tuzun Olsun needs hands six nights a week in Taksim

This is the most active drop-in humanitarian team found. A column dated **12 October 2026** reports that the association has handed out food to homeless people **every evening except Sunday for about 11 years**. It feeds **150–200 people a day, rising to about 300 at weekends**, and its volunteers are mainly **university students and office workers** ([Gazete Oksijen](https://gazeteoksijen.com/yazarlar/nurcan-baysal/binlerce-evsize-sadece-bir-avuc-dernek-sahip-cikiyor-286607)). Volunteers take on soup distribution in and around Gezi Park after work or school ([yemek.com](https://yemek.com/corbada-tuzun-olsun/)). Erasmus Student Network groups regularly join as teams, packing food and splitting into groups for the Taksim round ([ESN activity](https://activities.esn.org/activity/corbada-tuzun-olsun-19390)). That suggests a newcomer with limited Turkish can fit into a shift, though no source states a language policy. The team format, central location and the mix of ages and backgrounds make it a strong weekly or twice-weekly fixture for someone on the European side. Two details remain unverified. One search summary put service at **20:30–22:30**, but its source could not be pinned down. No confirmed sign-up link or Instagram handle was found either. Contact the association through its own channels before turning up.

### The European Games application closes 15 January

The **Istanbul 2027 European Games run 16–27 June 2027** with about 6,000 athletes ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_European_Games)). The official volunteer programme is run by the Ministry of Youth and Sports with the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality (İBB). It takes applicants **aged 16 and over** and has a stated **application deadline of 15 January 2027**. The site said applications would "open soon", but a later search summary suggested the form may already be live ([EG Volunteers · İstanbul 2027](https://volunteer.2027istanbul.org/)). The English Games site has a "Volunteer with us" section ([Istanbul 2027 EN](https://www.2027istanbul.org/en)), so this is probably the most English-accessible large programme on the list. Role lists, training schedules and language requirements have not been published, though. Apply through official channels only: istanbul27.com states that it is not one ([istanbul27.com](https://istanbul27.com/)). Multi-sport Games normally put volunteers into venue teams for an intense fortnight. That is excellent for meeting dozens of people at once, but it does not replace a weekly habit, which is why it is a second goal alongside a weekly team rather than the main plan.

### LÖSEV and TEGV onboard newcomers in cohorts

For Turkish speakers, these two children's charities suit someone who wants to start immediately and meet a steady group. **LÖSEV** (the Foundation for Children with Leukemia) accepts its "Gönüllü Ol" form online or in person. It **approves online applications within 24 hours** and invites new volunteers to **intro meetings held every 15 days** ([LÖSEV](https://www.losev.org.tr/tr/losev-de-gonulluluk)). Volunteer roles include representing LÖSEV at your workplace, joining awareness campaigns and supporting its "İnciler" education project ([LÖSEV EN](https://www.losev.org.tr/en/volunteering-at-losev)). Its Istanbul volunteers have an unusually visible social life: World Volunteer Day gatherings on both sides of the city ([TrHaberBurda](https://www.trhaberburda.com/losev-dunya-gonulluler-gununde-bulustu/)), group dance filming in Kadıköy, Karaköy and around Galata Tower ([Sancaktepe Ses](https://sancaktepeses.com/haber/losev-dunyayi-salliyor-27435)), and a volunteer choir that performs in the city ([Ulusehir](https://ulusehir.com/haber/losev-korosu-istanbulda-sahne-aldi-212157)). World Volunteer Day falls on 5 December, so a newcomer who signs up this week could be at a gathering within a fortnight. Membership dues are optional ([LÖSEV archive](http://st.losev.org.tr/tr/losev-de-gonulluluk)). The Istanbul office address was not found.

**TEGV** (the Educational Volunteers Foundation of Turkey) asks for volunteers who are **18 or older and high-school graduates**. Volunteering is free. Training is video content on TEGV Dijital followed by a **one-day live session** ([Ayancık Gazetesi](https://www.ayancikgazetesi.com/tegv-gonullu-basvurularini-baslatti-nitelikli-egitim-icin-sosyal-katki-cagrisi/94741)). Activity volunteers run sessions for children in IT, maths, science, reading, art and English at the activity point nearest to them ([Bilim Şenliği](https://www.bilimsenligi.com/tegv-gonullulerini-bekliyor.html/)). Before starting you sign a commitment form and hand in an ID copy and a **criminal-record certificate** ([TEGV](https://tegv.org/gonullu/gonullu-ol-tegve-katil/)), so allow a few weeks. Istanbul's education parks include Nusret Arsel, Sema ve Aydın Doğan and Ferit Aysan, and the headquarters is in Ataşehir on the Asian side ([VKV Ansiklopedi](https://ansiklopedi.vkv.org.tr/Kategoriler/Kurumlar/Desteklenen-Kurumlar/TEGV)). The only list of individual learning units found dates from 2016. TEGV publishes no minimum weekly hours. Its English-language activities are a possible niche for English speakers, and it has an English application page ([TEGV EN](https://tegv.org/en/become-a-volunteer)).

### Animal sanctuaries take walk-ins with no paperwork

**Kurtaran Ev** is the lowest-barrier option in the city. It says it "always" needs volunteers at its Hadımköy farm and its Beşiktaş cat-adoption area, lists dog walking as a task, and states that **no form is needed**: just come to the living areas, which are open **every day 12:00–17:00** ([Kurtaran Ev](https://www.kurtaranev.org/gonullu)). An optional volunteer form on its English subdomain asks about frequency and preferred days ([Kurtaran Ev EN](https://en.kurtaranev.org/forms)). Hadımköy is on the far western edge of the city, so a visit takes half a day. Closer alternatives exist. Fatih Municipality's **Yedikule shelter** describes itself as run jointly by staff and volunteers ([Fatih Belediyesi](https://www.fatih.bel.tr/tr/main/news/yedikule-hayvan-barinaginda-yenileme-calismal/6114)), and its named volunteer group was active in December 2025 ([X post](https://x.com/nevzatcicek/status/2000563036126425454)). The only contact details found for it, though, come from a web page dating to around 2012. On the Asian side, a Kadıköy Municipality staff member said in 2026 that its shelter is open to volunteer visits **seven days a week** ([Bianet](https://bianet.org/haber/kadikoy-belediyesinin-topladigi-uc-kopek-hayvan-bakimevinde-318772)). The context matters. A 2024 law sends stray dogs into municipal shelters, and the Istanbul governor reported **46% collected by May 2026** ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/istanbul/istanbul-collects-46-of-stray-animals-moves-toward-full-control)). Shelters that accept volunteers probably need walkers more than ever, and you should expect animal-rights politics to come up in conversation.

## English speakers have fewer doors, but the right ones are open

Yabangee, the long-running English-language community site, warns that **most volunteering in Istanbul is primarily in Turkish**. For English speakers it points to **Istanbul&I** and AIESEC ([Yabangee](https://yabangee.com/finding-your-place-in-istanbul-a-practical-guide-for-foreigners-to-make-friends/)). Istanbul&I has been active since 2016. It runs clean-ups, language-exchange nights, art projects with refugee children and Ramadan meals, and its events are generally open to English speakers. Its volunteers skew toward university age and recent graduates ([Yabangee](https://yabangee.com/finding-your-place-in-istanbul-a-practical-guide-for-foreigners-to-make-friends/); [Borgen Magazine](https://www.borgenmagazine.com/istanbuli/)), and the contact address is contact@istanbulandi.org.tr ([Istanbul&I](https://www.istanbulandi.org.tr/)). No 2025–2026 activity was confirmed, so message it before planning around it. AIESEC is student-focused.

For women, **International Women of Istanbul (IWI)** is the most clearly active option. It is open to foreign women with a foreign passport or a Turkish residence permit and costs **2,000 TL a year** (effective September 2025) ([IWI](https://iwistanbul.org/joinus)). Its charity work includes helping at the **Bomonti Fransız Fakirhanesi** home for poor elderly people: spending time with residents, ironing, sewing and gardening ([IWI](https://www.iwi-tr.org/page-1863602)). A new board was elected at its June 2026 AGM ([IWI](https://iwistanbul.org/)). For a longer-term, networking-style commitment, two Rotary clubs use **English as their official language**: **Istanbul-Bosporus**, which meets at the Swissotel in Beşiktaş ([Rotary](https://my-cms.rotary.org/en/club-profile/AA5A3A55-C1C3-473B-ADFE-6C93FA344A4E?embed=true)), and **Istanbul-Adalar**, based in Kadıköy ([Rotary](https://my-cms.rotary.org/en/club-profile/16EAF7AE-B83B-4C0F-BC02-EAF4929D1F4D?embed=true)). Both probably skew older and professional. The **Union Church of Istanbul** near İstiklal Avenue runs **weekly social programmes for refugee mothers with young children and clothing distribution** ([Union Church](https://ucistanbul.org/ministries)). Its congregation spans nearly 30 nationalities, with English services at 9:30 and 11:15 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Union_Church_of_Istanbul)), and visitors report being invited to lunch afterwards ([Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293974-d3628340-Reviews-Union_Church_of_Istanbul-Istanbul.html)). It is the best English-language route into regular refugee support, though there is no published sign-up process. Darülaceze, the state elderly-care institution, has an English volunteer application page ([Darülaceze EN](https://www.darulaceze.gov.tr/en/visit-and-volunteering/volunteering-application)), but its residents speak Turkish. InterNations and most Meetup groups are purely social ([Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/internations-istanbul-expat-community/)). They are better for finding a friend to bring along than for finding volunteering itself.

Two constraints apply to non-citizens. Several state channels are closed to them outright. AFAD's disaster-volunteer system requires **Turkish citizenship** ([Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/afad-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-kimler-basvurabilir-sartlar-nedir-h-4703794)), as does membership of any JCI chapter in Turkey ([JCI Maltepe](https://jcimaltepe.org/jci-sik-sorulan-sorular/)). Whether foreigners can register on Kızılay's portal or the ministry platforms was not confirmed. The legal position is also unclear. Turkish law requires foreigners to get a work permit before they start "working" ([ÇSGB, Law 6735](https://live.csgb.gov.tr/Media/lcohhstd/6735-sayılı-uluslararası-işgücü-kanunu.pdf)), and Turkish legislation does not explicitly define volunteering ([DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/4772595)). No official guidance on unpaid volunteering by residence-permit holders was found. A reasonable reading, which is an inference rather than legal advice, is that occasional unpaid help with a registered association is low-risk, while a timetabled, job-like role is closer to the line. Ask the organisation how it handles foreign volunteers.

## Bigger commitments pay off in tighter communities

Once a weekly habit is in place, several options offer deeper belonging in exchange for more onboarding. Almost all of them need Turkish.

| Option | Why it builds community | Commitment | Language | Side / area | How to join |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Darülaceze (state elderly care) | Reading, music, handicrafts and theatre with residents; group events on Fridays | Flexible, supervised by social workers | Turkish needed (English form) | European: Okmeydanı, Şişli | Online form |
| KAÇUV (children with cancer) | Child-activity teams on oncology wards and at family houses | Mandatory in-person intro meeting, then an evaluation | Turkish needed | European HQ (Harbiye); hospitals across the city | gonullu@kacuv.org |
| Türk Kızılay "Gönüllü Ol" | Team tasks, soup kitchens, disaster readiness | Online training before your first task | Turkish needed | City-wide; soup kitchens in Pendik (Asian), Fatih, Beylikdüzü, Bağcılar (European) | gonulluol.org |
| TEMA "Aktif Gönüllü" | Neighbourhood environmental teams | Ongoing; registration appears linked to a donation | Turkish needed | City-wide | gonullu@tema.org.tr |
| GEA Arama Kurtarma (search and rescue) | Annual 3-month seminar cohort; a "social activities only" track | 3 months and up | Turkish needed (English web pages) | Asian side (216 phone number) | Online form |
| AKUT (search and rescue) | A team you belong to for years | 3–6 months of basic training; about 2 years to become field-ready | Turkish needed | HQ in Şişli; teams across the city | Watch for the next intro meeting |
| Spor İstanbul race crews | Station teams at the Half Marathon and Marathon | Training twice a year, plus race day | Mixed | European (Ataköy training) | Account at event.spor.istanbul |
| Habitat Derneği volunteer trainer | Cohort weekends to train as a trainer | 1–2 trainings a month | Turkish needed | City-wide | Watch its newsletter for calls |
| Bird counts / IKGT birding trips | Small weekend crews; winter shearwater counts | Seasonal | Mixed | Both sides (Bosphorus) | ikgt.org |

**Darülaceze** is the best recurring option with elderly people. It is a central-government institution in Okmeydanı, separate from İBB's own Darülaceze in Kayışdağı. Anyone **over 18** can volunteer, and activities run under social workers' guidance ([Darülaceze SSS](https://darulaceze.gov.tr/ziyaret-ve-gonulluluk/sss)). Areas include reading and conversation, music, handicrafts and theatre, and cognitive support ([Darülaceze](https://www.darulaceze.gov.tr/ziyaret-ve-gonulluluk/g%C3%B6n%C3%BCll%C3%BCl%C3%BCk-basvurusu)). Group events take place on **Fridays with prior approval**, and the site is closed on Mondays ([Darülaceze visits](https://www.darulaceze.gov.tr/ziyaret-ve-gonulluluk/ziyaret-basvurusu)). **KAÇUV** requires an application form, then a **mandatory in-person intro meeting in Istanbul**, then an evaluation ([KAÇUV](https://www.kacuv.org/gonulluluk/nasil-gonullu-olabilirsiniz)). Child-activity volunteers then work on paediatric oncology wards and in family houses ([KAÇUV](https://kacuv.org/bireysel-gonulluluk/)). Ward work is meaningful but emotionally heavy. If the breakup is still raw, LÖSEV's events and campaigns side or a running team is a gentler start. **Kızılay**'s platform passed **500,000 registered volunteers** in 2026 ([Haber 1](https://www.haber1.com/guncel/turk-kizilayin-gonullu-ol-platformunda-uye-sayisi-500-bini-asti)). Volunteers work in 33 areas, and assignment depends on finishing basic first-aid and disaster trainings ([DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/kurumsal/turk-kizilayin-gonullu-sayisi-500-bini-asti-2970279)). Recent Istanbul tasks appeared in Esenyurt, Fatih, Üsküdar, Avcılar and Beylikdüzü ([gonulluol.org](https://gonulluol.org/VolunteerDuties)). Kızılay's IFRC-accredited **16-hour first-aid course** is also a useful first step, and it is social in its own right ([DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/kurumsal/turk-kizilayin-ilk-yardim-egitimine-uluslararasi-tescil-2467620)).

Disaster preparedness, sharpened by the **April 2025 M6.2 Marmara earthquake** after which AFAD applications quadrupled ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/afad-volunteer-applications-quadruple-following-istanbul-quakes-208819)), produces the closest-knit groups of all. **AKUT** starts with an intro meeting, followed by **3–6 months of basic training**. Becoming field-ready takes **about two years** of performance tests and advanced training ([Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/pazar/akut-gonullulerinden-nasil-kurtarici-olunur-6134240); [Eleman.net](https://www.eleman.net/is-rehberi/pratik-bilgiler/akut-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-sartlari-nelerdir-h10782)). Applications are paused from time to time ([Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/akut-gonullusu-olmak-icin-ne-yapmak-gerekir-nasil-akut-gonullusu-olunur-3562877)). AKUT does say there are tasks everyone can do without joining operations ([AKUT bulletin](https://www.akut.org.tr/docs/e-bulten/AKUT-e-bulten-temmuz-2014.pdf)). **GEA** runs **3-month volunteer seminars every year** ([GEA](https://gea.org.tr/?p=13562)). Its form lets you choose **social activities only**, search and rescue only, or both ([GEA form](https://form.jotform.com/251274534971057)), which makes it a rare low-pressure way into a rescue community. If you want to train with neighbours instead, district schemes exist, but some limit who can join. Kadıköy's disaster-volunteer programme requires **Kadıköy residence** ([Afet Kadıköy](https://afet.kadikoy.bel.tr/projelerimiz/kadikoy-afet-gonullusu-projesi)). In Beylikdüzü, residents who finished a four-week training became neighbourhood disaster volunteers ([TRT Haber](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/mahalle-gonullulerini-dogal-afetlerde-bilincli-mudahale-icin-egitiyorlar-509258.html)). Your muhtar (the elected neighbourhood head) is the place to ask about the neighbourhood-volunteer (MAG) model.

**Spor İstanbul** runs volunteer training **twice a year at the Ataköy Olympic House** for Marathon and Half Marathon volunteers and gives a certificate ([Spor İstanbul](https://spor.istanbul/?p=5006)). You create an account at event.spor.istanbul and fill in the "Gönüllü Ol" form. It is open to ages 16 and up, and members are notified before places fill. The 2026 marathon window ran **10 August to 12 October** ([Maraton İstanbul](https://maraton.istanbul/spor-istanbul-gonulluleri/)). **Habitat Derneği**'s volunteer-trainer cohorts are competitive: **35 of 135 applicants** were taken in April 2026 ([Habitat newsletter](https://habitatdernegi.substack.com/p/habitat-dernegi-nisan-2026-bultenine)). Forms ask for **one or two trainings a month** ([Jotform](https://form.jotform.com/262113567843964)), and its monthly newsletter was still publishing in October 2026 ([Habitat](https://habitatdernegi.substack.com/p/habitat-dernegi-ekim-2026-bultenine)). Winter is shearwater season for birders: Yelkouan counts on the Bosphorus **peak in February** ([Bianet](https://bianet.org/yazi/yelkovan-kuslarinin-pesi-sira-162988)), and the Istanbul Bird Watching Society (IKGT) runs weekend trips where newcomers learn the basics ([Posta](https://www.posta.com.tr/magazin/cumartesi-postasi/hayati-kuslari-izlemekle-geciyor-209619)). Cyclists can train as sighted "pilots" with **Eşpedal**, riding tandem bikes with visually impaired co-pilots ([otuzbeslik](https://www.otuzbeslik.com/yazilar/espedal-engelli-ve-engelsiz-kisileri-bulusturuyor)). Turkish speakers who want a steady stream of new calls should follow the Sivil Sayfalar volunteer listings ([Sivil Sayfalar](https://www.sivilsayfalar.org/gonullu/)). The ministry's new **İyilik Var** platform is also worth watching: it launched on **12 September 2026** and reached 20,000 volunteers in its first month ([Net Haber](https://www.nethaber.com.tr/haber/iyilik-var-platformuna-bir-ayda-20-bin-gonullu-katildi-1024968)), though its eligibility rules were not found.

## Cross three famous names off your list

The research notes contradicted each other on three well-known organisations. In each case the newer and more specific evidence wins.

**Small Projects Istanbul: treat as closed.** One note listed it as the clearest English-friendly refugee option. That was because its Olive Tree centre page in Çapa still describes volunteer-taught Turkish classes, a women's craft collective and children's activities ([SPI](https://www.smallprojectsistanbul.org/community-education/)), and because 2017 coverage describes an expat-heavy team ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/expat-corner/2017/01/10/volunteering-in-istanbul-ways-to-do-good-by-helping-refugees)). The organisation's own volunteer page, however, says that **after eight years it brought operations to an end** ([SPI – Volunteer](https://www.smallprojectsistanbul.org/volunteer/)). It was founded in 2013 ([Ockenden Prizes](https://www.ockendenprizes.org/2020-prizes/small-projects-istanbul/)), which suggests it closed around 2021. Its Go Overseas listing has also been inactive for more than six months ([Go Overseas](https://www.gooverseas.com/lead/219758/inquiry?program=219759)), and the researcher who called it active found no 2025–2026 evidence of new volunteers being taken on. A specific closure notice outweighs a program page that was never taken down.

**Tarlabaşı Toplum Merkezi (TTM): not a working volunteer route.** Another note recommended TTM as the most English-friendly children's option. That recommendation rested on a Yabangee page ([Yabangee](http://yabangee.com/volunteer/)) and on TTM's own application page, which still refers to a "2020/II" call ([TTM](https://www.tarlabasi.org/gonulluluk)). Both predate events. The centre was **sealed in October 2024** for "unlicensed activity" ([Evrensel](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/530386/tarlabasi-toplum-merkezi-muhurlendi)). The dissolution case reached its 12th hearing on 21 January 2026 ([bianet](https://bianet.org/haber/tarlabasi-toplum-merkezine-acilan-fesih-davasi-ertelendi-315847)). An appeals court then ruled that the association had "ended by itself", and the file went to the Court of Cassation. A search summary dated that ruling **22 April 2026** ([bianet](https://bianet.org/haber/tarlabasi-toplum-merkezi-hala-kapanmadik-dayanismaya-devam-ediyoruz-320523)), while a FIDH piece dated September 2026 describes the court declaring TTM "legally non-existent" ([FIDH](https://www.fidh.org/en/region/europe-central-asia/turkey/turkey-appellate-court-ruling-against-tarlabasi-community-center)). TTM itself says "we are still not closed" and will use every legal avenue, and Amnesty has called the proceedings judicial harassment ([bianet/Amnesty](https://bianet.org/haber/tarlabasi-toplumunu-destekleme-dernegine-yonelik-yargisal-tacizlere-son-verilmeli-311959)). Supporters can follow its statements, but there is no open intake to plan around.

**Ahbap: avoid.** Founder Haluk Levent and 13 others were **jailed on 16 July 2026** in an investigation into alleged misuse of earthquake donations ([Turkish Minute](https://turkishminute.com/2026/07/16/turkish-court-jails-rock-star-haluk-levent-13-others-in-ahbap-charity-investigation/amp/)). A court made the state's savings insurance fund, TMSF, its **trustee** ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/court-appoints-trustee-to-ahbap-charity-amid-misconduct-probe-224922)). Activities were suspended and assets frozen ([Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/ahbap-sorusturmasinda-haluk-levent-dahil-18-kisi-tutuklandi-mal-varliklarina-el-konuldu-7624793)), and a dissolution suit was filed ([NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/galeri-ahbap-dernegine-yonetim-kayyumu-atandi-1736330)). These are allegations, not a verdict, and Levent's side denies them. Even so, the network that once made Ahbap Istanbul's most social volunteer scene has stopped operating, and you should check any group recruiting under its name.

Several smaller cautions apply too. **Refugee volunteering is thinner than older guides suggest**: UNHCR says 2025 funding cuts forced it and its partners in Türkiye to "drastically scale down" community-based activities ([UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/unhcr-annual-results-report-2025-turkiye.pdf)), and the professional refugee NGOs surveyed had no public walk-in volunteer programmes. **Unofficial portals** need care. One note found ibbgonulluleri.org collecting personal and address details without appearing to be an official İBB site ([ibbgonulluleri.org](https://www.ibbgonulluleri.org/gonulluol/)), while that site's own "who we are" page says it works with İBB's mayoral office ([İBB Gönüllüleri](https://www.ibbgonulluleri.org/bizkimiz/)). Verify it through an official ibb.istanbul page before handing over your address. **Feeding street dogs on your own** in parks or near schools now breaks a November 2025 governor's circular ([Turkish Minute](https://www.turkishminute.com/2025/11/24/istanbul-governor-bans-uncontrolled-feeding-of-stray-dogs-cites-health-risks/)), although one monitor found no verified fine for feeding alone ([Dog Desk](https://www.dogdeskanimalaction.co.uk/p/istanbuls-stray-dog-feeding-ban-the)). Go with an established group instead. **Istanbul Pride** is not a safe volunteering route: it has been banned every year since 2015, and at least 50 people were detained at the June 2026 march ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/europe/article/3358674/police-turkey-detain-dozens-istanbul-pride-event-after-authorities-banned-march)). Finally, if you are past your mid-twenties, skip **youth-only programmes**. TOG currently works only with university students ([TOG](https://www.jotform.com/TOG/bizekatil)). İBB's Genç Gönüllü lists ages **15–25** ([İBB](https://gencgonullu.ibb.istanbul/)), although a 2023 call cited 18–30.

## Conclusion

Istanbul's volunteering scene has changed in a way older guides miss. The two grassroots hubs that used to absorb newcomers, Ahbap and Tarlabaşı, have been sidelined by court action. The best-known English-language refugee charity has closed, and international funding for refugee programmes has shrunk. What remains is more structured and less political: Kızılay, LÖSEV, TEGV, municipal sports volunteering and, most usefully, charity running, which links almost every major NGO to one shared training calendar. For someone rebuilding a social life, that structure helps. A cohort intro meeting or a Saturday training run gives you a reason to come back that does not depend on an invitation, and coming back is what turns acquaintances into friends.

The second takeaway is about timing and language. 2027 is an unusually full year for Istanbul, with the European Games, Formula 1's return and the 19th Istanbul Biennial ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/chinese-curators-to-lead-19th-istanbul-biennial-in-2027-221597)), and the first deadline falls within eight weeks. The way to use it is to treat the big events as recruiting grounds for a weekly habit, not as substitutes for one. Language is the hard limit on everything else: most of the deeper-commitment options here assume Turkish. A non-Turkish speaker should start with a running team, a soup round or a sanctuary and take a Turkish class alongside. By the time the Marathon volunteer window opens next August, much of the second tier will be within reach.
