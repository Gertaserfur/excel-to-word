# Animal Welfare Volunteering in Istanbul (in person, as of late 2026)

> Method note for the report writer: research done 2026-11-21 with web search only. Direct page fetches were blocked in this environment for kurtaranev.org, yabangee.com and yedikulehayvanbarinagi.com, so the details for those organizations come from search-engine extracts of their pages, not a full read. Many grassroots sources are several years old; each item below is flagged with its apparent age. Treat all schedules as "confirm on Instagram/WhatsApp before going".

## Q1. Which shelters (private and municipal) accept individual walk-in or scheduled volunteers, and what can volunteers do there?

### Takeaway
The most open, social and realistic options in late 2026 are the private sanctuary **Kurtaran Ev** (Hadımköy, European side; walk in daily 12:00-17:00, no form needed, dog walking allowed) and Fatih Municipality's **Yedikule shelter** (historic peninsula, European side), which is officially run as a public-volunteer partnership with an active "Yedikule Hayvan Barınağı Gönüllüleri" group. District shelters such as **Kadıköy** and **Sarıyer** do take volunteers, but mostly by appointment or through linked associations, and İBB (metropolitan) shelters route volunteers through a training course and a "Local Animal Volunteer Card". No source confirms open walk-in access at İBB shelters in 2026.

### Inferences
- Kurtaran Ev is the single clearest "show up and help" option, and the English subdomain suggests some English-language support, but Hadımköy is on the far western edge of the city (Arnavutköy area), so it is a half-day trip; carpooling with other volunteers is likely how people get there (not confirmed).
- Yedikule is the most central shelter with an active, named volunteer group in 2025 and a municipality that explicitly wants volunteers; it is probably the best "recurring weekend shift" option on the European side if the contact details still work.
- Kadıköy shelter is the best Asian-side option; the 2026 Bianet quote about 7-day volunteer visits is the most recent evidence of access found.
- Because the 2025-2026 street-dog roundups are filling municipal shelters (see Q4), demand for walkers/socializers at shelters is likely higher than ever, but access depends heavily on each municipality.

### Gaps
- No current (2025-2026) confirmation of volunteer rules at Beşiktaş or Şişli municipal shelters; a third-party directory surfaced by search listed a phone number for a Beşiktaş sterilization/rehab center, but it could not be verified and is omitted.
- Could not confirm whether İBB's volunteer card course is currently running, its schedule, or whether İBB shelters accept walk-ins.
- Could not verify Kurtaran Ev's minimum age, language requirements, or transport options; page fetch was blocked.
- Yedikule's contact details come from a decade-old page; current Instagram handle for "Yedikule Hayvan Barınağı Gönüllüleri" not found.
- BUPAWS location and current activity not established.

## Q2. Which NGOs and grassroots groups run group-based, recurring volunteer activities (feeding networks, forest dogs, islands, horses, wildlife, expat groups, adoption/sterilization drives)?

### Takeaway
The most social recurring formats are (a) the **Beykoz forest dog feeding rounds** on the Asian side (Huysuz İhtiyar, Patika/Göksu animal lovers, Orman Çocukları), which go out several times a week and openly ask for more hands; (b) the **Adalar volunteer networks** (Ada Gönüllüleri Derneği, Adalar Platformu); (c) English-friendly expat-led groups (**Tails of Istanbul**, **Cihangir Cool for Cats**, Help A Stray Cat); and (d) occasional large group days (İBB's 4 April events, adoption fairs such as MÜŞTEREK at Bomontiada). **HAYTAP** takes volunteers through a form plus training. Horse volunteering on the islands is effectively gone, and no public wildlife-rehab volunteering program in Istanbul was found.

## Q4. Legal/political context 2024-2026: how the stray-animal law and Istanbul directives affect volunteering

### Takeaway
Law No. 7527 (in force 2 Aug 2024) obliges municipalities to collect stray dogs into shelters and allows euthanasia in defined cases; the Constitutional Court upheld it in 2025. In Istanbul, the Governor ordered a roundup before schools reopened (Aug 2025), banned "uncontrolled feeding" of dogs in sensitive public places (Nov 2025), and pushed municipalities to clear all street dogs by end of May 2026 (46% collected by May 2026; a later 15 Oct 2026 deadline reported). Street-dog feeding is now legally grey and politically fraught, shelters are crowded, and some advocacy groups allege volunteers are denied access to municipal shelters; cats and private sanctuaries are less affected.

- Realistic now: shelter-based volunteering (private sanctuaries and cooperative municipalities like Fatih/Kadıköy), cat feeding/sterilization, fostering and flight escorting. Riskier or less certain: street-dog feeding in parks/near schools (directive), forest feeding during fire-season bans, and expecting open access to large İBB/governor-driven facilities.
- With large numbers of dogs moved into shelters in 2025-2026, shelters that accept volunteers likely need walkers and socializers more than before; this is arguably where a newcomer adds the most value while meeting regulars.
- Volunteers should expect activist/political conversations in these communities; protests are a social but sensitive part of the scene.
