## 1. AKUT Arama Kurtarma Derneği (Istanbul teams): how to join, training, time commitment, fitness, support roles

### Takeaway
AKUT is the best-known civilian search-and-rescue NGO and works like a close-knit team you belong to for years. You start at an intro meeting ("gönüllü tanışma toplantısı"), then do a basic training phase of about 3 to 6 months. Becoming operational (field-ready) takes about 2 years and needs performance tests and health checks. You must be 18 or older, and the whole process is in Turkish. AKUT says openly that it needs "not Rambos" and has tasks anyone can do, so non-field support volunteering is possible. However, no 2026 application call or date was found, and older guides say applications are sometimes paused.

### Cited Findings
- **Process:** you attend a volunteer intro meeting (described as monthly), then finish the required basic trainings and paperwork, and only then does your volunteering start. — [Habertürk AKUT guide](https://www.haberturk.com/akut-gonullusu-olmak-icin-ne-yapmak-gerekir-nasil-akut-gonullusu-olunur-3562877)
- **Application status:** the same guide says applications were "temporarily stopped" and that forms for the next intro meeting would be posted through a link announced later. The guide's date is unclear (it refers to the Feb 2023 earthquake), so this may be out of date. — [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/akut-gonullusu-olmak-icin-ne-yapmak-gerekir-nasil-akut-gonullusu-olunur-3562877)
- **HQ address:** Büyükdere Caddesi No:120, Esentepe, Şişli, Istanbul. People outside Istanbul can check akut.org.tr to find their nearest team. — [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/akut-gonullusu-olmak-icin-ne-yapmak-gerekir-nasil-akut-gonullusu-olunur-3562877)
- **Age:** anyone aged 18 or older can attend an AKUT volunteer intro meeting in their region. This comes from AKUT how-to guides; the search summary did not say which one. — [Eleman.net](https://www.eleman.net/is-rehberi/pratik-bilgiler/akut-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-sartlari-nelerdir-h10782); [Habertürk explainer](https://www.haberturk.com/akut-nedir-acilimi-ne-demek-akut-gonullu-olma-sartlari-akut-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-basvurusu-nasil-yapilir-3564452)
- **Basic training:** covers communication, ethics, first aid and disaster awareness. One source says it takes about 6 months and another says 3 to 6 months. — [Milliyet Pazar, "AKUT gönüllülerinden: Nasıl kurtarıcı olunur?"](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/pazar/akut-gonullulerinden-nasil-kurtarici-olunur-6134240); [Eleman.net](https://www.eleman.net/is-rehberi/pratik-bilgiler/akut-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-sartlari-nelerdir-h10782)
- **Fitness and exams:** to join the operational team you must pass various performance tests and health checks, then do advanced training. The whole path takes about 2 years. No sources mention a written "exam" after the intro meeting. — same sources as above ([Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/pazar/akut-gonullulerinden-nasil-kurtarici-olunur-6134240); [Eleman.net](https://www.eleman.net/is-rehberi/pratik-bilgiler/akut-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-sartlari-nelerdir-h10782))
- **Meeting frequency (conflicting):** some articles say intro meetings are monthly and others say every six months. — search summary of the AKUT guides above
- **"Not Rambo" (2019):** AKUT president Recep Şalcı said AKUT needs volunteers who can work in every area, not superheroes, and that volunteers become disaster-ready after 2 years of training. — [TRT Haber (2019)](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/yasam/akut-rambo-degil-her-alanda-is-yapacak-gonulluler-ariyor-441130.html)
- **Support roles:** AKUT's own bulletin says joining operations is not mandatory, that AKUT needs all kinds of people, and that there are tasks everyone can do. In practice this means non-field support work. — [AKUT e-bulletin, July 2014](https://www.akut.org.tr/docs/e-bulten/AKUT-e-bulten-temmuz-2014.pdf)
- **Unpaid:** all AKUT work is voluntary and unpaid. — [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/akut-gonullusu-olmak-icin-ne-yapmak-gerekir-nasil-akut-gonullusu-olunur-3562877)
- **İBB + AKUT "Afet Gönüllüleri" project (older):** run by İBB's disaster coordination centre (AKOM) with AKUT. AKUT trainers aimed to teach 5,450 Istanbul residents how to organise assembly areas and give first response by the end of 2022, and a drill was held at Maçka Demokrasi Parkı. — [Hürriyet topic page](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/haberleri/bb-afet-gonulluleri-projesi); [Sözcü (2022)](https://www.sozcu.com.tr/2022/gundem/ibb-ve-akuttan-ortak-tatbikat-7523808/)
- **Free trainings with İBB:** a Cumhuriyet headline says "AKUT organised Istanbul for the earthquake; Turkey is next", and the search summary says free disaster trainings started with Istanbul as the pilot city. The date was not visible. — [Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/turkiye/akut-deprem-icin-istanbulu-orgutledi-sira-turkiyede-1998300)

### Inferences
- AKUT is the most "tight community" option of all, because people train together for months to years. It is also the biggest commitment and needs Turkish.
- Someone who doesn't want the 2-year field path can still ask at the intro meeting about support or logistics roles (the "tasks everyone can do").
- Since applications are opened and paused from time to time, the practical first step is to follow AKUT's site and Instagram and register for the next intro meeting.

### Gaps
- No 2026 or 2027 intro-meeting dates or open application call were found. The official volunteer page (akut.org.tr) could not be fetched.
- No exact weekly hours were found, and no official fitness standard (for example a run test) was published in the sources.
- No English-language track was found. It is unclear whether non-Turkish citizens can join.
- The list of Istanbul sub-teams and districts was not retrieved.

## 2. AFAD Gönüllülük Sistemi (state disaster agency volunteer platform)

### Takeaway
AFAD's volunteer system is free and open all year. You sign up on e-Devlet, which sends you to gonullu.afad.gov.tr, and training comes in three levels: Temel (basic, online), Destek (support, face-to-face and field training given by the AFAD provincial office) and Uzman (expert). It requires Turkish citizenship (T.C.). Sources disagree on the minimum age: 15 for basic, 18 for support. Applications quadrupled after the 23 April 2025 Silivri earthquake (M6.2). It works well as a structured entry point, but on its own it is less of a "community" than AKUT or GEA unless you also join an AFAD-accredited volunteer team.

### Cited Findings
- **How to apply:** search for "AFAD Gönüllülük Sistemi" on e-Devlet. You are then sent to gonullu.afad.gov.tr by SMS and email. — [Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/afad-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-kimler-basvurabilir-sartlar-nedir-h-4703794); [Yeni Şafak (2)](https://www.yenisafak.com/afad-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-sartlar-neler-h-4505355)
- **Eligibility (conflicting):** Yeni Şafak says any T.C. citizen aged 15 or older can join. Another summary says 15 or older for the basic level and 18 or older for Destek (support) training. ListeList says 18. A volunteer quoted by Yabangee said "anyone between 18-60". — [Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/afad-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-kimler-basvurabilir-sartlar-nedir-h-4703794); [Politikam (2025)](https://www.politikam.com/afad-gonullu-basvuru-nasil-yapilir-2025-sartlar); [ListeList](https://listelist.com/afad-hakkinda-afad-gonullu); [Yabangee, "Be Earthquake Ready in Istanbul"](https://yabangee.com/?p=29938)
- **Health:** you must be fit for field work and travel. — [Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/afad-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-kimler-basvurabilir-sartlar-nedir-h-4703794)
- **Structure:** there are online, face-to-face and field trainings, at three levels (Temel, Destek, Uzman). Face-to-face training is given by the AFAD provincial directorates (İl Müdürlükleri). — [Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/afad-gonullusu-nasil-olunur-kimler-basvurabilir-sartlar-nedir-h-4703794); [DergiPark academic article](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/4448763)
- **Istanbul example (2025):** a Marmara University notice says people who finished the online course were invited to face-to-face field training. A "Destek AFAD Gönüllüsü" schedule assigned Istanbul districts, including Maltepe, to groups for sessions in October 2025. — [Marmara Univ. Destek AFAD Gönüllüsü programme PDF](https://mato-aef.marmara.edu.tr/dosya/aef/mato/resimler/Destek%20Afad%20Gonullusu%20Egitim%20Programi.pdf)
- **Cost:** a volunteer quoted by Yabangee said the training is "completely free". — [Yabangee](https://yabangee.com/?p=29938)
- **Istanbul volunteer teams:** the number of AFAD-accredited volunteer teams in Istanbul reached 139, with about 5,000 accredited volunteers. Nationwide, 51 teams and 2,141 members were accredited in one year. The article date was not shown. — [TRT Haber](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/afad-baskani-pehlivan-bir-yil-icerisinde-51-ekip-2-bin-141-uye-akredite-oldu-944102.html)
- **Surge after April 2025:** before the 23 April 2025 Silivri/Marmara earthquake, AFAD averaged about 200 new volunteer applications a day nationwide. That rose to about 800 a day (fourfold), and 5,480 people applied in the period after the quakes. — [Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/afad-volunteer-applications-quadruple-following-istanbul-quakes-208819); [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/afad-volunteer-applications-rise-fourfold-after-istanbul-earthquakes/news); [Karar](https://www.karar.com/guncel-haberler/afada-gonullu-basvurusu-4-kat-artti-1956241)
- **Total volunteers:** more than 1.6 million nationwide by September 2025 (a May 2025 report said about 1.59 million). — [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/afad-expands-its-volunteer-force-to-over-16m-nationwide/news); [Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/afad-gonullu-ordusu-16-milyonu-asti-4749031); [Yeni Şafak, "iki milyona koşuyor"](https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/afad-gonulluleri-iki-milyona-kosuyor-4749229)
- **Volunteer dogs:** AFAD also trains volunteers' dogs for disaster response in Istanbul. Only the headline was seen. — [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/istanbul/afad-trains-volunteer-dogs-for-disaster-response-in-istanbul)
- **Accreditation:** NGO and municipal volunteer teams can be accredited by AFAD (there is a guide on the accreditation process). — [AFAD Akreditasyon guide PDF](https://akreditasyon.afad.gov.tr/upload/Node/42911/files/Akreditasyon_Basvuru_Sureci_Rehberi.pdf)

### Inferences
- AFAD is not an option for foreigners or non-citizen residents, because sources say T.C. citizenship is required.
- For meeting people, the online Temel level is solitary. The social value starts at the face-to-face Destek training and grows further if you join one of the ~139 accredited Istanbul teams (most are NGO or municipal teams such as AKUT, GEA and district units).
- The April 2025 surge means training classes may be oversubscribed, so it helps to sign up early and watch the AFAD Istanbul provincial directorate's announcements.

### Gaps
- No 2026–2027 Istanbul face-to-face training calendar was found.
- Exact current age rules were not confirmed from an official AFAD page (sources conflict between 15, 18 and 18–60).
- Training is assumed to be in Turkish; no English option was found.

## 3. Other disaster and emergency volunteer routes in Istanbul (GEA, İBB/AKOM, district municipalities and "Mahalle Afet Gönüllüleri", Kızılay, UMKE, Ahbap, TOG and others)

### Takeaway
After AKUT, the strongest community-style options are **GEA Arama Kurtarma** (an Istanbul-based SAR group with 3-month volunteer seminars and a "social activity only" volunteer track), **municipal disaster volunteer teams** (Kadıköy's BAK and "Kadıköy Afet Gönüllüsü", Beşiktaş's PUSAR, and the neighbourhood-based "Mahalle Afet Gönüllüleri" model that starts at your muhtar), and **Kızılay** (the Gönüllü Ol platform with 33 role types, plus a 16-hour accredited first-aid course). UMKE is essentially for health professionals only. **Ahbap is not a viable option right now:** in July 2026 a court appointed a trustee and suspended all its activities.

### Cited Findings
**GEA Arama Kurtarma**
- GEA runs voluntary 3-month seminars every year, with theory and drills at set intervals. — [GEA "Join GEA Team"](https://gea.org.tr/?p=13562)
- The online "Gönüllü Ol" form lets you choose **social activities only**, **search and rescue only**, or both. You must confirm you have read the volunteer declaration (taahhütname). — [GEA volunteer form (Jotform)](https://form.jotform.com/251274534971057); [GEA Declaration of Volunteering](https://gea.org.tr/en/declaration-on-volunteering/)
- GEA's 2025 activity report lists basic and advanced SAR trainings as well as AFAD trainings. — [GEA 2025 Faaliyet Raporu](https://gea.org.tr/GEA_2025%20_Faaliyet_Raporu.pdf)
- Contact: +90 216 342 48 48 (a 216 number is on the Asian side of Istanbul), gea@gea.org.tr. The site has English pages. — [GEA English home](https://gea.org.tr/en/home-en/)

**İBB (Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality) / AKOM**
- İBB AKOM and AKUT ran the "İBB Afet Gönüllüleri" assembly-area training project, which aimed to reach 5,450 residents by the end of 2022 (see section 1). No 2025–2026 call was found. — [Hürriyet topic page](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/haberleri/bb-afet-gonulluleri-projesi); [Sözcü](https://www.sozcu.com.tr/2022/gundem/ibb-ve-akuttan-ortak-tatbikat-7523808/)

**District municipalities and neighbourhood teams**
- **Kadıköy:** the municipality's search-and-rescue unit, BAK Kadıköy, trains residents, who become "Kadıköy Afet Gönüllüsü" (Kadıköy disaster volunteers). Many residents answered the municipality's call and became volunteers. — [Karar](https://www.karar.com/sehir-haberleri/6-subattan-mahallelere-uzanan-dayanisma-bak-kadikoy-tarafindan-verilen-2027169)
- **Beşiktaş:** the municipality's Afet İşleri Müdürlüğü (disaster affairs directorate) ran an earthquake drill with PUSAR (Beşiktaş Belediyesi Arama Kurtarma Derneği, the Beşiktaş municipal SAR association). — [DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/gundem/besiktas-belediyesi-olasi-istanbul-depremine-karsi-tatbikat-gerceklestirdi-2700206)
- **How the "Mahalle Afet Gönüllüleri" (neighbourhood disaster volunteers, MAG) model works:** ask your muhtar whether your neighbourhood has a MAG team. If not, request one through the muhtar and the district governorship (kaymakamlık). A group of about 50 people can apply to the MAG foundation for training. MAG-DER was founded in 2017. The search summary drew on several sources without clear attribution; likely ones include [Posta column, "Kurtarma gönüllüsü olmanın tam zamanı"](https://www.posta.com.tr/yazarlar/mustafa-bakacak/kurtarma-gonullusu-olmanin-tam-zamani-2626445) and [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/4400932). Verify before citing.
- **Other Istanbul MAG examples** in the same summary (attribution unclear, dates unknown): İstanbul MAGDER teaches basic disaster awareness in schools ([haberler.com](https://www.haberler.com/istanbul-daki-okullarda-deprem-seferberligi-4561261-haberi/)); a Şişli MAG association trains municipal MAG volunteers; in Beylikdüzü hundreds applied, and people who finish a 4-week training become neighbourhood disaster volunteers ([TRT Haber, likely source](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/mahalle-gonullulerini-dogal-afetlerde-bilincli-mudahale-icin-egitiyorlar-509258.html)).
- **Model elsewhere (not Istanbul):** Karşıyaka (İzmir) takes MAG applications through its municipal website and muhtars, with 16 hours of training. It shows what to expect. — [Karar](https://www.karar.com/sehir-haberleri/karsiyaka-afet-gonullulerinde-4-donem-kayitlari-basladi-2077836)

**Türk Kızılay (Turkish Red Crescent)**
- The "Gönüllü Ol" platform (gonulluol.org) launched in 2020 and has more than 500,000 registered volunteers. There are 33 role areas, including disaster and emergency, first aid and social aid. Online trainings prepare volunteers, and what you are assigned depends on finishing basic first-aid and basic disaster trainings. — [DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/kurumsal/turk-kizilayin-gonullu-sayisi-500-bini-asti-2970279)
- **First-aid course:** Kızılay's 16-hour certified course covers 13 topics with theory and practice and is accredited by the IFRC's Global First Aid Reference Centre. You book it by applying to a Kızılay First Aid Training Centre (32 centres in its branches nationwide). — [DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/kurumsal/turk-kizilayin-ilk-yardim-egitimine-uluslararasi-tescil-2467620)
- Kızılay gave first-aid training to nearly 70,000 people in 2025. — [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/kizilay-provides-first-aid-training-to-nearly-70000-people-in-2025/news)
- **Price (low reliability):** a 2026 guide gives 9,500–10,500 TL for a 16-hour basic first-aid course in general. This is not a Kızılay or Istanbul price, and the page shows signs of AI-generated text. — [Haber Gazetesi](https://www.habergazetesi.com.tr/ilk-yardim-kursu-nedir-kimler-almali-suresi-ve-2026-fiyat-bilgileri)
- **Certificate validity:** first-aid certificates are valid for 3 years. — [BOEnstitü](https://boenstitu.com/blog/ilk-yardim-sertifikasi-nedir-ne-ise-yarar-nasil-alinir)

**UMKE (Ulusal Medikal Kurtarma Ekibi, the national medical rescue team)**
- You join by individual written application. Members are chosen from health staff working for the Ministry of Health. Health staff from other public or private institutions can also join but pay for their own uniforms and equipment. One report says civilians can become UMKE volunteers in a support capacity. — [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/umke-nedir-katilim-nasil-saglanir-umke-gonullusu-olmak-icin-yapilmasi-gerekenler,ON66fVJPak2A20Vj2QQbbQ); [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/galeri-umke-nedir-iste-umkenin-acilimi-41650616)
- Members must complete UMKE basic training. UMKE has about 20,698 volunteer personnel (1,787 doctors, 17,568 health staff, 1,343 technical staff). The figure is from a news report; the most likely source is the Ministry page. — [Ministry of Health](https://saglik.gov.tr/TR-105481/ulusal-medikal-kurtarma-ekibi-umke-21-yasinda.html)

**Ahbap (suspended)**
- In July 2026 an Istanbul prosecutor's investigation led to the arrest of founder Haluk Levent. Reports put the number of arrests at 14, 18 or more. A court-appointed trustee (kayyum) was installed, all activities were suspended as a precaution, assets were frozen, and a case to dissolve the association was started. These are allegations and precautionary measures, not a final verdict. — [Yeni Asır, 16 Jul 2026](https://www.yeniasir.com.tr/yasam/2026/07/16/son-dakika-haluk-levent-dahil-14-supheli-tutuklandi); [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/galeri-ahbap-dernegine-yonetim-kayyumu-atandi-1736330); [Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/ahbap-sorusturmasinda-haluk-levent-dahil-18-kisi-tutuklandi-mal-varliklarina-el-konuldu-7624793); [Medyascope, 20 Jul 2026](https://medyascope.tv/2026/07/20/ahbap-sorusturmasi-bugune-kadar-neler-oldu/)

**Other trainers and routes**
- **TOG (Toplum Gönüllüleri Vakfı, a youth volunteering foundation):** in 2025, 1,161 young volunteers in 32 cities took part in its local disaster-volunteering trainings. Groups can request a local training through a form. — [TOG request form](https://form.jotform.com/250123272340038)
- **Güvenli Yaşam:** offers free earthquake-readiness training in three sessions: basic safety, first aid, and volunteer search and rescue. — [Yabangee](https://yabangee.com/?p=29938)
- **Interpreters:** during the 2023 earthquake response, volunteer interpreters were in high demand because foreign rescue teams could not call out to survivors in Turkish. Expats in Üsküdar helped aid groups. — [AA](https://aa.com.tr/en/turkiye/translators-plug-critical-communication-gap-in-turkiye-quake-relief-efforts/2824089); [TRT World](https://trtworld.com/article/12797356)
- **Consulate seminars:** the Philippine Consulate in Istanbul ran a disaster-preparedness seminar for its citizens in May 2024, with AFAD-linked and municipal speakers. — [DFA Philippines](https://dfa.gov.ph/dfa-news/news-from-our-foreign-service-postsupdate/34673-empowering-communities-philippine-consulate-general-in-istanbul-organizes-disaster-preparedness-seminar-for-filipinos)

### Inferences
- For "meeting neighbours", the neighbourhood-based MAG model and district teams (Kadıköy BAK, Beşiktaş PUSAR) are the most local and social. You train with people from the same few streets.
- GEA's "social activity only" option is a rare low-barrier way into a SAR community without committing to the field.
- **Language:** every disaster programme found works in Turkish. GEA's English pages and the interpreter role are the only English-adjacent openings. Non-Turkish speakers would most realistically start with first-aid courses or Güvenli Yaşam trainings (language not confirmed) and with event volunteering (section B).

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 calls were found for İBB/AKOM, Beşiktaş, Maltepe or most district programmes. People need to check their own municipality's site or ask their muhtar.
- No official Kızılay Istanbul course dates or fees were found, and it is unknown whether foreigners can register on gonulluol.org.
- The MAG Vakfı's current website and sign-up page were not found.
- GEA's minimum age, fitness requirements, fees and next seminar intake date were not found.
- No organised "CERT-style" programme in English was found anywhere in Istanbul.

## 4. Istanbul Marathon and other Spor İstanbul races: volunteer programme and dates

### Takeaway
Spor İstanbul (İBB's sports company) runs a standing volunteer programme for its races. Volunteers staff stations along the route, and specialists run trainings twice a year at the Ataköy Olympic House, with a certificate at the end. The **48th Istanbul Marathon was on 1 Nov 2026** with about 46,000 participants, so it has already passed. The next big Spor İstanbul race is the **Istanbul Half Marathon on 18 April 2027** (date from race aggregators). No published volunteer application deadline was found; you contact Spor İstanbul through its "Become a Volunteer" page.

### Cited Findings
- **The role:** Spor İstanbul's "Become a Volunteer" page describes a sports volunteer who "supports and motivates the athletes with a smiling face, joy and energy at the stations" at its events through the year. — [Spor İstanbul, Become a Volunteer](https://spor.istanbul/?p=5006)
- **Training:** "Training courses are held at Ataköy Olympic House, twice a year, led by specialists, for volunteers who take part in the Istanbul Marathon and Istanbul Half Marathon. Participants receive a certificate." The page gives a contact email (the snippet looked garbled, so check the page). — [Spor İstanbul](https://spor.istanbul/?p=5006)
- **Satisfaction surveys:** Spor İstanbul's 2025 Social Impact and Satisfaction Report has a section evaluating the experience of Istanbul Marathon volunteers. Only the table of contents was visible. — [Spor İstanbul 2025 report PDF](https://spor.istanbul/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sosyal-Etki-ve-Memnuniyet-Raporu-2025.pdf)

## 5. Adım Adım (charity running community): how it works and why it is social

### Takeaway
Adım Adım is a volunteer-run charity-running movement. Runners enter Istanbul races (mainly the Istanbul Marathon in November and the Half Marathon in April) and each raises donations for an NGO through the İyilik Peşinde Koş (İPK) platform at ipk.adimadim.org. It is very social because runners join NGO, company and running-group teams that train and race together. Its scale is large: 67 NGOs raised about 166 million TL at the 2025 marathon. For 2026 the marathon campaign window ran from 5 Oct to 16 Nov 2026, so the next chance is the **18 April 2027 Half Marathon**.

### Cited Findings
- **Model:** runners raise donations for an NGO, donors give through the İPK platform, and donations can be tracked live. People who don't run can support by donating to runners' campaigns. — [Ekonomist](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/haberler/kosarak-40-stkya-113-milyon-topladilar-3932); [Diken](https://www.diken.com.tr/?p=101813)
- **Steps (ÖRAV, an education NGO):** companies form teams of volunteer runners. About 4 weeks before the race, runners log in to ipk.adimadim.org, add their company's team name and create donation campaigns. Race registration is separate, at maraton.istanbul. — [ÖRAV, Yardımseverlik Koşusu](https://www.orav.org.tr/yardimseverlik-kosusu)
- **2026 cycle:** adimadim.org lists the Istanbul Marathon on 1 Nov 2026 (6.5K corporate/fun run, 15.5K, 42K), plus the Antalya Ultra Marathon and the Istanbul Half Marathon. Donation campaigns opened on ipk.adimadim.org from 5 Oct and closed on 16 Nov 2026. — [adimadim.org](https://adimadim.org/); [Races page](https://adimadim.org/iyilik-pesinde-kostugumuz-yarislar/)
- **Darüşşafaka:** the deadline to register as a Darüşşafaka runner (15K/42K) was 13 Oct, and corporate-run registration closed on 4 Aug 2026. Contact: adimadim@darussafaka.org. At the 2025 marathon, 820 individual and corporate volunteers ran for Darüşşafaka. — [Darüşşafaka](https://www.darussafaka.org/bagislariniz/bagis-kanallari/yardimseverlik-kosusu)
- **2025 (47th marathon):** 67 NGOs raised 166,119,000 TL in total. The figure came from a search summary; this is the most likely source. — [Ekonomist, "İyilik rekoru kırıldı"](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/haberler/istanbul-maratonu-nda-iyilik-rekoru-kirildi-68076)
- **Earlier year:** 10,073 runners ran for 36 NGOs and raised 9.7 million TL. — [Ekonomist](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/haberler/maratonda-stklara-yine-bagis-yagdi-6002)
- **Running groups:** groups exist as teams on the İPK platform. For example, the "Adım Adım Ankara" running group raised 51,400 TL for the 2026 Istanbul Half Marathon. — [İPK team page](https://ipk.adimadim.org/team/view?id=1444)
- **Other charity-run routes:** TOG and the Half Marathon both run charity-run programmes. — [TOG charity runs](https://www.tog.org.tr/en/charity-runs); [Istanbul Half Marathon charity run](https://istanbulyarimaratonu.com/en/charity-run/)
- **Background:** [Wikipedia, Adım Adım Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad%C4%B1m_Ad%C4%B1m_Initiative)

### Inferences
- **Why it is social:** you pick a cause, join that NGO's runner team (or your company's or a running club's), train together over the weeks before the race, fundraise among friends, and race in matching team kit. That gives a natural group and repeat contact over two seasons a year (April and November).
- **Next action:** pick an NGO and register for the Half Marathon at istanbulyarimaratonu.com before price tiers rise (pricing runs until 12 April 2027). Then open an İPK campaign when Adım Adım opens the Half Marathon cycle, probably about 4 to 6 weeks before 18 April 2027.
- **Language:** the platform and most NGO teams work in Turkish. The Istanbul Half Marathon has English race pages.

### Gaps
- adimadim.org could not be fetched. No 2027 campaign opening dates, Adım Adım volunteer (non-runner) roles, or Istanbul group-training schedules were confirmed.
- No minimum fundraising target was found.
