# Market & Community Size: Stardew Valley Mobile Modding (as of Oct 12, 2026)

Method note: Research used web search snippets only. Direct page fetches (Nexus Mods, PocketGamer.biz, Stardew wiki) failed because of DNS errors or a 403 from the proxy. Figures marked "(snippet)" came from search summaries of the cited page and could not be checked on the live page. Estimates are marked **[EST]**.

## 1. Player base: total sales, mobile share, mobile store metrics, mobile revenue

### Takeaway
The latest official total is **41M+ copies (Dec 2024)**: 26M on PC and 7.9M on Switch. Mobile is not broken out. No newer official total turned up through Oct 2026. Mobile is probably a mid-single-digit-million slice **[EST]**. The Google Play listing shows a **5M+ install** tier (snippet). Sensor Tower's stale public snippets suggest mobile earns roughly **$0.3M/month in the US** **[EST]**.

### Cited Findings
- Announced Dec 2024: over 41 million copies sold on all platforms, with 26M on PC and 7.9M on Nintendo Switch. Barone gave no breakdown for mobile, PlayStation or Xbox — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/stardew-valley-sales-grew-to-over-41-million-by-2024-s-end); [The Flagship Eclipse, Dec 30 2024](https://www.theflagshipeclipse.com/2024/12/30/stardew-valley-developer-reveals-how-the-game-has-sold-across-various-platforms/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/stardew-valley-sells-over-41-million-copies)
- Roughly 7.1M copies are unattributed (41 − 26 − 7.9). Outlets *speculate* that mobile is a "significant portion" of that remainder — [Gaming Amigos](https://www.gamingamigos.com/post/stardew-valley-41m-sales); [Twisted Voxel](https://twistedvoxel.com/stardew-valley-sales-have-surpassed-41-million-copies/). AllKeyShop gives about 6M for Xbox and PlayStation, which looks like its own estimate — [AllKeyShop](https://www.allkeyshop.com/blog/stardew-valley-sold-41-million-copies-news-d/)
- No official sales total newer than 41M turned up in 2026 searches. Raijin estimates about 20M copies on Steam alone and about $194.4M gross Steam revenue (data as of July 4, 2026, third-party, medium confidence) **[EST]** — [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/413150/Stardew_Valley)
- Google Play listing: **5M+ downloads, 4.6★** (snippet, undated; current at the time of search) — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chucklefish.stardewvalley&hl=en_US)
- iOS and Android together passed 1M downloads (Chucklefish, ~2019–2020) — [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=255399). Android launched in 2019 at $7.99 — [Droid Life](https://www.droid-life.com/?p=226898)
- iOS grossed $1M in the 3 weeks after its Oct 2018 launch — [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/stardew-valley-revenue-1-million)
- Sensor Tower public overview estimates (snapshots about 660 days old, so roughly late 2024): **US Google Play about 30k downloads and $90k revenue per month; US App Store about 50k downloads and $200k revenue per month** **[EST]** — [Sensor Tower Google Play](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/com.chucklefish.stardewvalley?country=us); [Sensor Tower iOS](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/1406710800?country=us)
- Steam concurrency (PC engagement proxy): all-time peak about 236k (third-party trackers); a 2026 peak of 123,002 on Jul 5, 2026; a 24h peak of 81,798 in the snapshot through Sept 23, 2026 (SteamDB snippet) — [SteamDB](https://steamdb.com/de/tools/steam-charts/413150-stardew-valley); [Tracker.gg](https://tracker.gg/population/steam/413150)

### Inferences
- US mobile estimates from Sensor Tower: Android about 30k/mo and iOS about 50k/mo. Android is the platform that can be modded, and it is the smaller US store. Worldwide Android share is likely higher than in the US, since Android dominates outside the US **[EST]**.
- If cumulative mobile units are about 5–8M worldwide **[EST, derived from the 5M+ Play tier plus iOS]**, the Android pool that could be modded is probably about 4–6M lifetime installs. Active players are a fraction of that.
- Mobile is a paid upfront game with no IAP. Revenue estimates therefore track new purchases only, and they say little about how engaged the installed base is.

### Gaps
- No official mobile unit count or Android/iOS split.
- The exact current Play tier (5M+ vs 10M+) and review count could not be checked live. App Store ranking and review count were not found.
- No AppMagic data and no lifetime mobile revenue figure was found.

## 2. PC modding scale (Nexus, SMAPI)

### Takeaway
Stardew is one of the top five or so games on Nexus. It has **about 32k mods and about 174k files** (snippet, 2026), and it had **about 290–296M mod downloads by early 2024**, when it ranked about #5 on Nexus. SMAPI has **385k+ endorsements** on Nexus and **3.1M downloads on CurseForge**.

### Cited Findings
- Nexus Stardew stats page: about **32,371 mods, 173,723 files, about 276M file views, about 15.5M endorsements**. The downloads field rendered as 0 in the snippet — [Nexus Stardew stats](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/about/stats)
- Early 2024: about 290M downloads, **5th among games on Nexus** — [WN Hub (Jan 2024, citing TheGamer)](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-42739). About 296.4M in Feb 2024 — [Multiplayer.it](https://multiplayer.it/notizie/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim-ha-generato-50-dieci-miliardi-download-nexus-mods.html). An undated Nixpkgs data file lists 25,015 mods and about 600.8M downloads. The metric is unclear, so treat it cautiously — [Nixpkgs commit](https://code.ornl.gov/nix/nixpkgs/-/commit/ce53d45bf5e95bc0c6184c3bf526bea577c28a95)
- SMAPI (Nexus mod 2400): **385,533 endorsements**. Unique and total download counts did not render in the snippet — [Nexus SMAPI](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/2400?tab=files)
- SMAPI on CurseForge: **3.1M downloads**, last updated Mar 14, 2026 — [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/stardewvalley/utility/smapi)
- 1.6 update (Mar 19, 2024) drove a then-record of 146,159 concurrent Steam players — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-43127)

### Inferences
- Nexus endorsements typically run at about 5–10% of unique downloads. On that basis, 385k SMAPI endorsements suggests roughly **4–8M unique SMAPI downloaders** **[EST]**. Compared with 26M PC copies, that implies roughly **15–30% of PC owners mod** **[EST]**.
- SMAPI for Android has 1,934 endorsements against SMAPI's 385k (about 0.5%). By the same ratio, Android SMAPI has roughly **20k–40k unique downloaders on Nexus** **[EST]**. GitHub and Discord distribution comes on top of that, so the real reach is likely higher.

### Gaps
- SMAPI unique download count (live Nexus page blocked).
- No official "% of players who mod" figure found.
- Current Stardew rank on Nexus in 2026 is unconfirmed.

## 3. Demand signals for mobile mods

### Takeaway
Demand is persistent but hard to quantify. A maintained Android SMAPI port exists, along with a launcher, a third-party Android mod manager, a dedicated Discord, wiki pages, a Nexus forum guide and multiple "best Android mods" articles. iOS modding is effectively impossible, and recurring forum threads ask for it. No quantitative YouTube, Reddit or Google Trends data was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- **SMAPI for Android** (Nexus mod 44436, by NRTnarathip): **1,934 endorsements**, v4.3.2, last updated Apr 1, 2026. Download fields did not render — [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/44436)
- Lineage of the port: MartyrPher (game 1.4) → ZaneYork (1.5) → NRTnarathip (1.6). Officially "experimental". Requires game 1.6.15+ (later 1.6.15.1) from the Play Store or Galaxy Store. Installed via a sideloaded SMAPI Launcher APK. Some mods don't work. Support is through a SMAPI-on-Android Discord — [Stardew Valley Wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Modding:Installing_SMAPI_on_Android)
- The launcher (NRTnarathip/SMAPILoader) is at v1.1.7, which fixed a crash on game 1.6.15.3. SMAPI builds ship in the Discord channel "smapi-beta" (latest 4.3.2.1 Beta) — [GitHub releases](https://github.com/NRTnarathip/SMAPI-Android-1.6/releases); [SMAPILoader releases](https://github.com/NRTnarathip/SMAPILoader/releases). Third-party APK mirrors carry old versions (v1.1.4, Dec 2025) — [APKPrime](https://apkprime.org/smapi-android/)
- A third-party **"QiMod Manager – Android Mod Manager"** exists on Nexus (mod 49124) — [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/49124)
- Community Nexus forum guide: "how to mod Stardew Valley on mobile devices" — [Nexus forums](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/13506427-guide-how-to-mod-stardew-valley-on-mobile-devices)
- iOS: SMAPI is "only fully supported on Windows, macOS and Linux and partially supported on Android". Users warn that iOS "workarounds" are often malicious. Recurring threads include "Will mods ever come to iOS" and "SMAPI for iOS devices?" — [Stardew forums](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/smapi-for-ios-devices.11309); [Stardew forums](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/will-mods-ever-come-to-ios.2772/latest)
- Pain point: the Android build is so different that "most modders don't even try to create an Android-friendly version". Users are told to look for Android-specific files — [Stardew forums](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/mods.46843/); [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/best-stardew-valley-mods-for-android/)
- Spam and SEO pages titled "Can you mod Stardew Valley on Android?" appear on hijacked domains (e.g. a calpoly.edu subdomain, fairware). This is a weak signal of search demand — [example](https://grandavehousing.calpoly.edu/news/stardew-valley-mods-on-mobile)

### Inferences
- Several independent tools (port, launcher, mod manager) and recurring guides suggest a niche of tens of thousands of active Android mod users **[EST]**. That is small next to millions of PC modders.
- The main friction points are sideloading, experimental status, crashes after game patches, and mods without Android support. Better tooling could reduce these.

### Gaps
- No YouTube view counts, Reddit thread frequency, Google Trends index or Discord member count could be retrieved. Live verification is recommended.
- No download counts for the Android SMAPI port.

## 4. Mods mobile players want most

### Takeaway
The mods named most often in Android guides are **Stardew Valley Expanded, Content Patcher (framework), Chests Anywhere, Automate, Tractor Mod, Generic Mod Config Menu and Farm Type Manager**. No ranked demand data was found for Ridgeside, CJB Cheats, Lookup Anything or UI Info Suite on mobile specifically.

### Cited Findings
- The Android port supports most SMAPI mods and content packs, including Content Patcher, Farm Type Manager, Json Assets and PyTK — [Stardew Valley Wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Modding:Installing_SMAPI_on_Android)
- Recurring picks for Android: Stardew Valley Expanded, Chests Anywhere, Better Ranching, Automatic Gates — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/best-stardew-valley-mods-for-android/); [PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/stardew-valley/best-android-mods/). Automate and Tractor Mod — [stardewguide.com](https://stardewguide.com/stardew-valley-mods/20-best-stardew-valley-android-mods); [Switchblade Gaming 2026](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/stardew-valley/best-mods-2026-smapi-guide/)
- Forum picks: Generic Mod Config Menu, Farm Type Manager, Portraiture, PyTKLite, AntiSocialNPCs — [Stardew forums](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/mods.46843/)
- On PC, SVE is described anecdotally as "the most popular mod by far" — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/413150/discussions/0/3192490350131102805)

### Inferences
- Mobile demand looks like PC demand: large content expansions (SVE, likely Ridgeside) plus quality-of-life mods (Chests Anywhere, Automate, Lookup Anything, UI Info Suite). UI mods are where Android compatibility breaks most often, because the mobile UI is custom **[EST]**.

### Gaps

## 5. Monetization options and community norms

### Takeaway
Monetization is constrained. Nexus **bans paywalled mods, early access, and commissions linked from Nexus**. It allows non-advertising Patreon or Ko-fi donation links and runs its own Donation Points / creator reward pool. ConcernedApe is "totally supportive of modding", but there is no Stardew paid-mod program. Community attitudes favor free mods and tools.

### Cited Findings
- Nexus policy prohibits linking to "mods that require payment to unlock via Patreon/Boosty/Ko-Fi", "restricting access to beta/early access builds to paying supporters", and commissions. Donation links are allowed only if they "must not advertise monetisation of modding". Discords that advertise mod monetization may not be linked either. The only exceptions are publisher-endorsed paid programs such as Bethesda Verified Creators — [Nexus Help: Donation Options & Guidelines](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/77-donation-options-guidelines); [Nexus Stardew news](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/news/14698)
- Nexus Donation Points: Nexus committed **$6,000–$10,000/month** of its own funds to an author donation pool and added a Patreon so users could top it up. Commenters criticized it as "scummy" and lacking transparency (older post, undated in snippet) — [Nexus news](https://www.nexusmods.com/hacknet/news/13642)
- Nexus framed the history around Valve's cancelled Skyrim paid-mods attempt and noted that author donations were "very small" — [Nexus news](https://www.nexusmods.com/onlyup/news/12564)
- Nexus ran a **Stardew Valley Modfest 2026** event with terms and conditions, which shows Nexus investing in the Stardew community — [Nexus Help](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/166-stardew-valley-modfest-2026-terms-and-conditions)
- SVE's author runs a Patreon (donation-style) — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/stardew-valley-43548452)
- ConcernedApe: "totally supportive of modding". He doesn't play mods himself. He coordinates update timing with SMAPI. He is "not opposed" to console mod support but doubts it's feasible because platform holders would need to approve — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/stardew-valley-doesnt-have-mod-support-on-console-for-numerous-reasons-says-creator-concernedape-but-hes-not-opposed-to-it-if-its-ever-possible/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/i-accept-the-blame-stardew-valley-creator-concernedape-address-broken-mods-after-recent-patch-says-he-will-release-fewer-large-patches-in-future/)
- ConcernedApe wants mods clearly distinguished from vanilla content — [X/ConcernedApe](https://x.com/ConcernedApe/status/1343040887895379968?lang=en)

### Inferences
- A paid or ad-supported launcher would conflict with Nexus norms. It could not be promoted on Nexus, and it would likely face backlash, especially if it redistributes free mods or SMAPI. The more viable models are a free tool with optional donations (Patreon or Ko-fi without perks tied to access), Nexus Donation Points, or a separate app-store product that does not resell mods **[EST]**.
- Mobile-specific legal risk: an ad-supported launcher that patches the commercial game APK could raise IP and ToS concerns with ConcernedApe/Chucklefish and Google Play policies **[EST; not verified]**.

### Gaps
- No found statement from ConcernedApe on paid mods or paid mod tools specifically.
- No data on ad-supported Stardew launchers or on earnings of Stardew mod authors (Patreon numbers).

## 6. Haunted Chocolatier timing & ConcernedApe plans

### Takeaway
**Haunted Chocolatier has no release date as of Oct 2026.** It is PC-first, with other platforms later. **Stardew 1.7 was announced on Aug 30, 2025** and also has no date. A 1.7 release would likely break mods and the Android port for a while, then renew interest.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 Haunted Chocolatier updates: Jan 29 (still in development), Jun 25 ("still grinding", 5 days/week on HC and 2 on Stardew), Aug 30 ("checking in", won't rush, no window). The "2030" rumor was debunked — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/haunted-chocolatier-news-update-2026/); [Game Rant Aug/Sep 2026](https://gamerant.com/haunted-chocolatier-august-september-2026-update/); [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/haunted-chocolatier-development-update-not-coming-out-in-2030/)
- Oct 12, 2026 interview: HC is "more of an action RPG" than a farming sim, and there is still no release date (single source, same-day report) — [My Nintendo News](https://mynintendonews.com/2026/10/12/concernedape-haunted-chocolatier-is-more-of-an-action-rpg-than-farming-sim/)
- HC is confirmed for PC only, with other platforms intended later — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/haunted-chocolatier/release-date)
- Stardew 1.7 was announced on Aug 30, 2025 at the Seattle concert. There is no date or estimate, and Barone said "it's going to be a while" (Sept 22, 2025) — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2025/09/stardew-valley-creator-announces-version-1-7-update); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/stardew-valley/stardew-valley-1-7-update-3244314)
- After 1.6.9 broke mods, Barone said he would "release fewer, large patches" — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/i-accept-the-blame-stardew-valley-creator-concernedape-address-broken-mods-after-recent-patch-says-he-will-release-fewer-large-patches-in-future/)

### Inferences
- Stardew stays actively supported, with 1.7 pending, so the installed base and modding interest should hold through 2027 **[EST]**. Updates to mobile game versions are a recurring compatibility risk for any Android mod tool. 1.6.15.x patches already forced launcher fixes.
- Haunted Chocolatier is PC-first and has no date, so it is unlikely to pull mobile players away in the near term **[EST]**.

### Gaps
- No release window for 1.7 or for when it reaches mobile. Nothing found on whether Haunted Chocolatier will support mods.
